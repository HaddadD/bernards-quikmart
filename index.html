The Laundromat

  • Spotless and well kept. Reviewers mention how clean the laundromat and washroom are, again and again.
  • Room to wait or fold. Tables and seating so you're not standing around.
  • Dryers that actually dry. Full-length dry cycles, so your clothes come out done.
